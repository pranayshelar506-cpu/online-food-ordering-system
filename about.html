THE HISTORY OF MUSAFIR CAFÉ

More Than Just
A Cup Of Coffee

Musafir Café was founded in 2026 by two close friends, Pranay and Anmol, who dreamed of creating a cozy place where people could relax, enjoy coffee, and share stories.

They opened the café in a small old shop and decorated it with wooden furniture, warm lights, books, and photographs from different journeys.

Soon, Musafir Café became a favorite place for students, travelers, artists, and friends.

The name "Musafir", meaning "traveler", reflects the idea that everyone is on a journey in life.

Today, Musafir Café remains a beautiful meeting place where coffee, conversations, memories, and journeys come together.
